About

hi there! i’m Jemima (but you can call me Mima) :)

i’m currently studying computer science and statistics at the university of toronto. my interests span applied ML, NLP, GPU programming, cognitive science, and linguistics.

growing up in jakarta and finding my way to toronto, i’ve come to love the feeling of being somewhere unfamiliar. i like throwing myself into things i know little about, figuring things out along the way, and discovering how much there is to learn when you stop being afraid of starting from scratch.
